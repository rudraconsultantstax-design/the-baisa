Our Story

Born in the vibrant lanes of Sanganer, Jaipur, The Baisa is a celebration of Rajasthan's rich textile heritage. For over 500 years, Sanganer has been the heartland of block printing and handloom weaving. We bring this legacy to modern women through contemporary designs rooted in tradition.

Every piece is handcrafted by skilled artisans using natural dyes and traditional techniques passed down through generations. From our workshop in Jaipur to your wardrobe, each garment carries the soul of Rajasthani craftsmanship.
